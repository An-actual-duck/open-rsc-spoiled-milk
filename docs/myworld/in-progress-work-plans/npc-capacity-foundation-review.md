## Engineering policy

The custom wire format must have an explicit new client version. Older custom
clients must be refused before entering an expanded world, not allowed to parse
new spawn bytes. Classic/retro profiles need explicit capacity compatibility
checks before entry; retaining packet bytes alone does not prevent aliasing.
Player registry budgets must remain unchanged. No gameplay, targeting priority,
RNG order, AI cadence, combat deadlines or signed-level authority change is in
scope. No map import, toolkit edits, server/client launches or deployment.

The final supported capacity/default remains subject to offline throughput,
allocation and memory measurements at approximately 4K/8K/16K populations and
Core private acceptance. Registration alone is not throughput evidence.
The 4047-placement tower requires a separately documented transient allowance.
Toolkit currently discovers the World initializer scalar and independently caps
the reviewed Limits family at 4000; moving the scalar into a contract requires a
paired, separately owned source-discovery update.
