Browser combat benchmark workload investigation
===============================================

Scope: private renderer benchmark harness and evidence validation only. No
production gameplay, public server, or original frozen fixture changes.

Observed manager-owned baseline-2: six non-null combat handles but no credited
ice/thunder/wood/ranged damage and no corresponding resource consumption.
Those measurements are partial-workload evidence, not full combat acceptance.

Diagnostic checkpoint
---------------------
The root cause remains unproven. Successful offline probes over the exact frozen
core/plugins/overlay and installed map exercise registered production event
queues, full terrain loading, full plugin discovery, ordinary player processing,
and source bootstrap loadouts. They prove the content IDs/resources can work;
they do not prove full Server.startServer initialization or real-clock behavior.
Early probe variants omitted normal logout processing or used a synthetic viewer
without an appearance, so they are exploratory evidence only. The corrected
appearance probe gives positive resources and contributions for all six channels.

The bootstrap now validates distinct registered actor UUIDs/indices, verifies
both the projectile path and adjacent action edge when choosing a placement,
checks rune insertion, and reports channel identity, exact scheduled-instance
membership, key presence, running status, execution count, actor registration,
coordinates, readiness/queued action, and explicit missing/orphan/stopped errors.
GameEventHandler.has alone is insufficient: it checks key presence, not identity.
The original damage ledger and real production attack events remain authoritative.
The fixture writes its detailed status, then fails closed after48ticks if any
expected family lacks real resources, contribution, or a running registered event.

Benchmark validation now requires before/after samples with advancing resources,
ledger contributions, and scheduler counts for every expected family, plus six
distinct live registered channels. Positive stale cumulative counters cannot pass.
No manual projectile emission or damage is added to combat-real.
