Scimitar / Anti-coagulant first implementation — November 13, 2026
=================================================================
Scope
-----
Add a single-use coating dose and permanent coated forms for all 14 current
scimitars. Acquisition is UNASSIGNED: no recipe, ingredient, shop, drop or player
starting item has been added. Moderator/private-test item ID: 3447. Coated forms
are 3448..3461, mapped in config/scimitar-coatings.json. Do not invent an
acquisition route while finishing the later god-crossbow effects.

The vial uses the existing native potion sprite with a dark red liquid mask.
Coated weapons retain their original inventory icons and effective held frames,
including material, classic/custom appearance, Worship restrictions, reserved
knight equipment points, equip levels, speed and other stats. No new art family.
One dose plus one unwielded, unnoted base scimitar -> coated scimitar + empty vial.
Wool plus an unwielded coated scimitar -> original scimitar; wool is consumed.
No right-click wipe action. Atomic inventory exchange rejects stale identities,
noted/wielded items and missing inputs without partially consuming anything.

Initial tuning (provisional, not final release balance)
-----------------------------------------------------
 - Hard primary hit threshold: 70% of that attack's snapshotted comparable max.
 - Require actual positive settled damage. A delivered critical qualifies.
 - One independent wound per source player/session and target lifetime.
 - Three pulses, one every 3 game ticks; no immediate pulse.
 - Tiers 1..4: Bleed1, independent inclusive 1..3 per pulse.
 - Tiers 5..8 (including black/white/grey): Bleed2, 3..5 per pulse.
 - Tiers 9..12: Bleed3, 5..7 per pulse. No tier-11 scimitar currently exists.
 - Reopening restores three remaining pulses without moving the next pulse.
   Keep the higher severity if different coated tiers reopen the same wound.
 - No per-hit proc chance in addition to the hard-hit roll.
 - Pulse damage uses the source player's existing Combat Luck system: an extra
   favorable candidate can change the roll, never duplicate the pulse.
 - Offhand, AoE/splash, poison, bleed, reflected damage and other secondary
   attacks cannot open wounds. No wound may be inferred from current equipment
   after the primary weapon/roll has been committed.
 - Messages are the owner's minor wound / wound / sizable wound lines and
   "You reopen the wound". Pulses currently use the standard red damage splat.

Bleed uses typed DOT settlement, player kill attribution and normal terminal
NPC handling. Nonmagical phase rules and final PvP safety apply to every pulse.
A player crossing into safety, source logout/death/session replacement, target
removal/death/respawn or signed-level change terminates that wound. Wounds are
short-lived runtime effects and are not saved into account data. Ordinary
poison remains a separate shared target state.

Scimitar damage roll
--------------------
Primary scimitar ceiling becomes ceil(original maximum * 0.90). Very small
maxima consequently remain unchanged. Coated and uncoated scimitars use the
same ordinary formula; the coating adds bleed rather than paying for base DPS.
Attack speed and equipment offense are unchanged. Existing offhand/secondary
roll formulas are unchanged and do not acquire the new primary wound metadata.

Preserve the original offense and defense draws, including existing miss chance.
For a positive hit conditional on its defense roll, let K be the old remaining
maximum and N=ceil(0.90*K). The old positive distribution is uniform on 1..K.
Map its quantile monotonically to a mixture of uniform 1..N and ascending
triangular weights 1..N, with triangular mixture q=3*(K-N)/(N-1). This preserves
(K+1)/2 expected damage exactly for ordinary unbiased rolls, still allows 1,
and raises the frequency of strong rolls relative to the smaller ceiling.
Retain existing roll biases and Combat Luck by mapping their original quantile.
The old scimitar family already had a 20% chance of an upward one-point shift;
this remains in place. Its existing advantage is slightly compressed as well.

A 20% ceiling reduction was prototyped but rejected during initial tuning:
combined luck/critical profiles lost too much uncoated damage. The 10% reduction
keeps ordinary damage much closer. At old M20, including the existing 20% weapon
bias, representative analytical expectations before percentage buffs are:
                    Defense0             Defense5             Defense20
 No luck/crit       10.690 ->10.668       7.870 ->7.860         3.420 ->3.415
 50% combat luck    12.352 ->12.116       9.452 ->9.366         4.265 ->4.230
 15% critical       12.087 ->11.767       9.690 ->9.381         5.907 ->5.603
 Both              13.986 ->13.374      11.707 ->11.212         7.628 ->7.174

These are uncoated profiles: bleed raises coated damage. A smaller maximum
necessarily reduces actual critical-hit power. Do not claim perfect DPS parity
for every crit/luck/buff/rounding build; this remains a balance-review limit.
Raw model, output and reproducible profile calculation are retained under
output/tests/scimitar-anticoagulant/. Actual gameplay and client checks use
maintained tests/myworld/test-scimitar-coatings.py with a synthetic native world,
dummy database config and no accounts, owner-map copies or public listeners.

Validation / integration
------------------------
Server core/plugin and standalone-client builds pass. Runtime checks cover all
14 dose/cleaning exchanges, held appearance/stat inheritance, stale-input
rejection, 24 ordinary damage/defense profiles (200,000 samples each), wound
ownership/timing/lifecycle, actual primary melee, phase, Wilderness border

and god-crossbow regression checks are retained. Their old total custom
animation-count assertion was updated from 1170 to 1173 for the preceding god

No public deployment, server interruption or release packaging is part of this
implementation. Browser delivery must be rebuilt at release time as usual.
