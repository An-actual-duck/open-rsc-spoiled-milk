NPC melee presentation follow-up — 2026-11-16

Owner reported intermittent missing melee retaliation/weapon swings during ranged
and magic testing, including a Moss Giant adjacent to the player. Live private
capture confirmed ordinary Giant melee attacks were committing and dealing damage;
this was not a global removal of melee. The owner also identified leash limits as
one contributing situation. Do not infer that every reported case had one cause.

A reproducible mixed-style presentation defect was found: the canonical projectile
cooldown controller called face(), selecting an idle/walking sprite and clearing
an active melee combat sprite. Preserve the current melee pose while holding that
cooldown when the same target still has an active NPC melee event. Dedicated
casters/shooters retain their projectile presentation; an actual Magic launch
switches away from melee and its cooldown does not invent a melee swing.

An adjacent-eligibility issue was found alongside it: checkAdjacentDistance checks
an edge/path and does not itself enforce distance. Canonical projectile selection
now also requires withinRange(target,1) before admitting melee. This avoids caching
a melee choice against a distant target with a valid projectile shot.
