Harralander coating recipes — current requirements
================================================
All three coating recipes use unfinished Harralander (457).

Weapon poison: level 23, ground blue dragon scale; 250 XP. Unchanged.
Anti-coagulant: level 33, one Sanguinary gland (3466) and one ground Naga scale
(3471); 275 XP; one single-use coating (3447). Level 33 was unused by
existing finished-potion recipes; level 38 already has Strong Antidote.
Viscous coating: level 46, one Cockatrice Eye (3342) and one Sticky Saliva
Gland (3341); 300 XP. Replaces its former unfinished Avantoe base (460).

Use any two anti-coagulant ingredients together with all three present.
Grind one raw Naga scale (3470) with a pestle and mortar into one ground
Naga scale (3471), following the blue dragon scale process. Both forms are
non-stackable and noteable. Nagas drop one raw scale at base chance 1/16.
Ingredients must be unnoted; one ground scale is consumed.
Both custom recipes retain Druidic Ritual, member-world and fatigue gates.
The exchange works with a full inventory and refuses stale/unsupported inputs
before consuming anything. Other ground scales stay in the inventory.

This supersedes recipe details in the historical battleaxe coating design
and implementation-review documents. Combat/application rules are unchanged.
No public deployment is part of this change.
