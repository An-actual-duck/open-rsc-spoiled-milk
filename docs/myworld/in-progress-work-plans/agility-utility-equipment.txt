AGILITY UTILITY EQUIPMENT — approved design and implementation
November 15, 2026

Scope
-----
Add three untradeable, unnoteable zero-stat equipment items. Pouches remain
tradeable. Normal weighted category/item/quantity rewards are unchanged.
Remove all shared rare-drop-table access. Broader resource-table redesign is
a later owner discussion, not part of this implementation.

IDs and unique odds per opened pouch
----------------------------------
3472 Boots of Swiftness: T1 1/1000, T2 1/500, T3 1/100.
3473 Gloves of Suregrip: T2 1/1000, T3 1/500.
3474 Cape of Traversal: T3 1/1000.
Unique rolls are independent and additional to normal pouch resources.
Duplicate ownership includes inventory, worn equipment and bank. Replacements:
boots -> Rune greaves (1994, the existing rune footwear); gloves -> Rune
gauntlets (1993); cape -> one random tooth/loop crystal-key half, equally likely.
Losing a piece allows another copy, but obtaining any copy never resets charges.

XP
--
Each worn piece adds 10%; the full set adds 30%, regardless of charge/combat.
Hook is central Skills.addExperience for positive Agility awards, including
quests and shared XP. Other skills/worlds are unchanged. Integer remainder
is retained in player cache, in hundredths of the engine's quarter-XP units.
Walking awards one displayed base XP (four internal units) through incExp,
so normal boosts, fatigue, XP freezing and server rates apply.

Boots
-----
100% initial charge, maximum100. Ordinary walking cadence is divided by1.5:
430ms -> 287ms; normal adjacent-step collision/path validation is retained.
Disable speed while any combat direction is active, and until ten seconds
after the final engagement ends. Existing recent-combat timers also apply.
No boot charge drains while speed is inactive, at zero charge, on blocked
steps, teleports, blink or scripted obstacle relocation.
Drain1% per50 tiles, plus20 with gloves and30 with cape: 50/70/80/100.
Fractional drain uses a common2800-unit denominator, preserving progress when
changing gear; toggling equipment cannot reset partial charge consumption.
Completed tier1/2/3 laps restore1/2/3% only while boots are worn.

Gloves
------
Uncharged. Standard courses, the Lumbridge/Al Kharid stepping stones and
agility shortcuts shorten explicit obstacle
waits by25%, carrying quarter-tick fractions through the obstacle sequence.
The cumulative wait is ceil(originalTicks*0.75), minimum one tick for a
nonzero obstacle. Dialogue/global plugin waits and quest cutscenes are untouched.
Legacy failures have a25% rescue chance:40% failure ->30%, not15%.
Configured shortcuts adjust their authored basis-point success threshold.
Required levels, failure damage, landing checks and obstacle order persist.

Cape
----
Starts with3 teleports, maximum3. Wear and walk2000 ordinary queued tiles to
regain one. Partial progress persists; a full cape banks no extra charge.
Cape charging counts ordinary walking in/out of combat. Walking XP requires
charged worn boots; combat suspends boot speed/drain, not the cape's walking
XP or recharge. All of these counters are player-owned, not ItemStatus.
Teleport menu: Gnome Stronghold, Barbarian Outpost, Wilderness and Yanille
Agility Dungeon. Validate the current arrival tile and normal teleport
restrictions after menu selection, then consume exactly one charge.
Normal restriction: no teleport at Wilderness20+, during a duel, or other
blocked quest locations / while carrying Ana. Cancelling costs nothing.

Visuals / compatibility
----------------------
Existing inventory silhouettes and wool-boots/wool-gloves/cape equipment art
use a coordinated0x65B8AC colour. No new image files or external art dependency.
Append-only appearances1190..1192 leave old IDs unchanged in both sprite modes.
Maintained client10060 adds actor-specific movement-rate opcode161 (four-byte
payload). The server sends it only to client10060+. Other actors/NPCs retain
their own normal rate, and existing movement packets remain byte-compatible.
Charges/counters survive removal, banking, death and logout via player cache.

Validation / release
--------------------
Offline fixtures use synthetic terrain plus exact installed course landing
geometry; no live accounts, listeners, database or public process is involved.
The public server and installed player clients are not deployed by this task.
A later release must publish the matching desktop/browser client builds.
Evidence: output/tests/agility-utility-equipment/ and output/reviews/agility-utility-equipment/.
