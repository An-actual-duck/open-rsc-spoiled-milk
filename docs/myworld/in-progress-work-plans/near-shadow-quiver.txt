Near-shadow solar quiver follow-up
=================================

Owner observation
The accepted distant-shadow update greatly improved fences at distance, but
nearby shadows still visibly shake/quiver, including with player/camera still.
This follows the distant-thin-shadow work at published cbd89824e.

Cause and focused correction
The near map was freshly built each frame using an absolute light-space
texel snap. As the sun rotates, dot(axis, world center) changes even though
the camera is stationary. At actual map coordinates around (64000,59000),
rounding that value crosses texel boundaries much more often than an otherwise
identical scene around the origin. The resulting grid phase hops change the
coverage of narrow alpha-cutout railing bars.

Near projection now retains a world-space grid center. Only camera displacement
relative to that center is snapped in the current sun basis. Sun rotation alone
no longer re-snaps the absolute map location. The tracking basis is normalized
in double precision and the world anchor is advanced using relative increments;
it is not repeatedly reconstructed from float-normalized absolute coordinates.
The established absolute far projection constructor is unchanged.

Near texture contents still rebuild every frame. There is no near depth history,
no actor caching, no filter widening, and no resolution/bias increase. High near
remains 2048 at radius32 tiles and Low is unchanged. Context/plane changes,
quality changes, suspend/Off and large 3D camera discontinuities reset the near
anchor. This also avoids carrying a phase across a vertical teleport.

CPU projection regression
20,000frames combine gradual sun movement and camera motion at the origin and
translated world location; projected relative geometry stays equivalent within
float matrix precision and the tracked center remains within a texel-scale
bound of the requested center.100,000fixed-sun frames require bit-identical
matrices and negligible anchor drift. Subtexel fixed-sun camera movement keeps
near XY rows stable. Existing sun-ray depth/Y-down ordering, rebasing, culling,
input validation and quality persistence remain checked.
