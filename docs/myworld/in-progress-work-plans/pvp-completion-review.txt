MyWorld Wilderness PvP completion — November 4, 2026

Agreed behavior

Player combat uses the same authored mechanics and values as PvE. Player
recipients must remain legally attackable in the Wilderness, including at
projectile impact and final damage settlement. Walking cancels the walker's
own attack; opponents can continue chasing. There is no walking immunity.

Explicit player attacks can affect other eligible enemy players. An NPC-primary
attack cannot acquire player collateral. Automatic and area targeting excludes
party and clan allies, including distinct Clan objects with the same stored ID,
and friends listed by the source player.
Traditional dueling remains disabled and its player-menu option stays hidden;
a future arena is separate work. The summon homage is named Inner Demon.

Implementation

Actor-owned melee, ranged, throwing and autocast retain independent targets.
Shared Slow, additive delays, stagger recovery, crits, coatings and mobility
use their PvE values. Retargeting preserves melee cadence, including unslowed
attacks. Both packet entry and ongoing combat bypass the old retreat immunity.

Scythe, shurikens, Iban/god spell splash, Thunder, chain lightning, Splinter,
Blood/Death robes and charged equipment admit filtered player recipients.
Shuriken volleys retain three targets and one ammo cost per projectile; pending
automatic throws recheck new party/clan/friend relationships. Terminal area effects
retain the actual death location despite immediate respawn relocation.

Cleric blessings work in Wilderness. Hostile casts are explicit attacks against
all eligible enemies in range, excluding party/clan/source-listed friends. God-slot precedence,
power curves, poison/summon/recoil weaknesses, crit/stagger/execution and phase
behavior remain shared. Player curses use the existing animation pipeline.
Non-corporeal players receive the existing translucent rendering via a new
player appearance record. Client/server version 10059 is required for the
paired release; generated schema-5 spell/effect definitions remain aligned.

Summons assist against legal player enemies and revalidate their owner at
impact. Victims retaliate toward the owner. Support summons absorb player
attacks using their normal values. Kills resolve to the owner. Player deaths,
loot ownership, kill records and equipment kill resources use the existing
once-only death transition. Melee kill XP is preserved without granting stale
melee XP for projectile or summon kills.

The final damage boundary blocks player-owned damage outside Wilderness,
including queued hits, procs, recoil and poison. Existing NPC/environmental PvE
damage continues there. Poison cleanses normally without delayed catch-up.
Both maintained MyWorld configurations now enable the completed Wilderness
combat path; publication/activation is a separate manager operation.

Coverage limits

Automated fixtures exercise production packet handlers, events, settlement,
wire encoding and client rendering. They do not establish human PvP balance,
latency fairness, or how multiple remote clients feel in play. The single-client
private setup is intended for the owner's final gameplay/visual check before
live activation. Arena duels are intentionally deferred, as requested.

Private-playtest corrections: Unify and friendly targeting

Unify and Greater Unify now teleport once to the existing safe pull destination
(one/three tiles closer, stopping within two), preserving range, costs and safe
landing selection. The explicit world and signed floor are preserved. They no
longer emit forced walking steps. The guide descriptions reflect teleportation.

Friends, party members and clanmates always receive a lower-priority Attack
menu entry, below non-combat actions even when no ground action is available.
Enemy level-based priorities remain unchanged. Client roster checks use current
member counts so removed members cannot be mistaken for current allies.

Hostile area/automatic effects use only the source player's friend list in
addition to shared party/clan checks. Adding another player protects that player
from your effects; it never prevents their effects from hitting you. Queued
automatic projectiles recheck this rule at impact, as do hostile Cleric casts.
Explicit primary attacks remain available via the secondary menu.

and source-owned friendship cases in the 40-scenario PvP suite. Existing 149
combat scenarios, 387 Cleric runtime assertions and Cleric cast-transaction
