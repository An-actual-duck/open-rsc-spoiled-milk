normal ammo power/effects/consumption/recovery. No automatic reserve on equip.

Zamorak bleed — owner-directed application design
------------------------------------------------
Bleed opens a wound from a sufficiently hard hit, rather than stacking poison-
like potency or using a separate generic proc roll. Proposed starting threshold
is damage >=70% of that attack's maximum hit (70% remains tentative). Compare
integers using damage*100 >= maximum*70, equivalent to ceil(maximum*0.70).
A qualifying ordinary hit opens a wound; a critical maximum roll also qualifies.
The RNG comes from the damage roll itself, without another chance-to-bleed test.
A wound delivers three bonus damage pulses, a finite extra-damage payload.
Exact payload size and pulse timing are not yet agreed: prior 25%-of-hit and
four-tick suggestions remain suggestions, not approved balancing values.
No poison-like hit accumulation is desired. Reapplication while a wound is
already active still needs a final rule; recommendation is to let the existing
three pulses finish without stacking or refreshing from further hard hits.

Implementation must compare roll and maximum on the same basis, snapshot the
maximum for that attack at launch, and only apply a wound after a successful
positive primary hit. Blocked/suppressed/missed attacks must not bleed. Avoid
comparing a modified/mitigated final hit against an unrelated unmodified max;
preserve the intended synergy that a delivered critical maximum roll qualifies.
Existing AttackRoll retains critical identity but has no maximum field; the
comparable maximum needs deliberate launch/settlement plumbing, not inference
from the equipped weapon after a projectile is already in flight. Secondary
bleed pulses must not recursively open wounds or trigger more weapon effects.
Saradomin and Guthix now have owner-directed mechanics recorded below; the
earlier defense-bypass Revelation and reactive-damage Briar Seed suggestions
are superseded.

Guthix — Briar Seed (owner-directed)
------------------------------------
"Imbed briar seeds into your enemies that explode into thorns every 5 shots
for extra damage and may hit nearby enemies as well."
After five resolved primary hits, including zero-damage hits, trigger one extra
AoE hit on the target and eligible nearby enemies within a one-tile radius.
Bonus damage is an RNG roll from 0 through 3, inclusive. The centre target gets
one extra hit, not both a centre hit and a duplicate splash hit. Start a new
five-hit cycle after the burst. No secondary burst hit may add another seed or
recursively trigger another burst. Invalid launches/blocked interactions are
not resolved zero-damage hits and must not create seeds.
Recommendation pending confirmation: count seeds separately for each attacker/
enemy pair, since the seeds are embedded in that enemy. Do not silently count
shots across five different victims. Independent 0..3 rolls per burst victim
are a proposed implementation detail; the owner has specified the cap and AoE,
not whether victims share one roll. Follow ordinary AoE eligibility, signed
level and PvP/party/clan/friend safety rules without bespoke exceptions.
This is the unique effect of Guthix's Thorned Bolts while its prayer is active.

Saradomin — Revelation (owner-directed)
---------------------------------------
While using the bow/prayer, build familiarity with the current enemy type.
Begin at 0%, gain one percentage point per kill, and cap at 15%. Continuing
against other individuals of the same enemy type retains the accumulated bonus.
Switching from zombies to giants resets it and starts building against giants.
Enemy families/types must not be individual spawn UUIDs; variant grouping needs
an explicit reviewed mapping. Existing Monster Slayer family groups are useful
input but are not a complete world-wide taxonomy. Do not infer solely from names.
The bonus skews damage rolls upward rather than raising the maximum hit.
It is lost only by unequipping the weapon, deactivating the prayer, or choosing
a different enemy type. No idle timeout or per-kill reset. Matching-cape changes
alone must not discard the familiarity while the bow/prayer remain active.
Credit qualifying kills once through the authoritative kill/reward path.

Exact roll-bias math is pending an owner clarification: recommended lifting the
minimum roll by the learned percentage of maximum, leaving maximum unchanged;
e.g. at maximum20 and familiarity15%, a pre-defense roll range of3..20 rather
than0..20. This interpretation has been asked but NOT approved as of this note.
It must not be silently implemented as +15% maximum damage, crit chance or luck.
This is the unique effect of Saradomin's Divine Bolts while its prayer is active.

God crossbows — design and implementation outline, November 13, 2026
====================================================================

Owner requirements
------------------
Add one god crossbow to each of Saradomin, Zamorak and Guthix's devotion artifact
reward pools. Use their god maces as the visual theme references. Equipping the
weapon reserves 80 prayer points; shots require no bolts or other ammunition.
The effective weapon should match a tier-11 crossbow with approximately tier-6
bolts, subject to balance review.

Proposed initial balance
------------------------
All three start with identical combat statistics and no additional god-specific
proc. The current Dragon crossbow (tier 11) has 38 rangedOffense and weaponSpeed
4. Mithril bolts (tier 6: tin, copper, bronze, iron, steel, mithril) have 12
rangedOffense. Use a self-contained 50 Ranged offense, speed 4, normal crossbow
range, one-handed mainhand equipment like existing crossbows. Recommend 80
Ranged and 80 Worship to equip, matching the existing top crossbow and artifact
Worship gate. Match the wielder's worshipped god as existing god weapons do.
Do not grant an extra prayer bonus that offsets the intended 80-point reserve.
These are starting recommendations, not yet implemented values.

The reserve is a persistent allocation, not an 80-point payment on equip and
not a per-shot drain. Count it in both server and client prayer allocation so
prayers cannot also spend it. Free it immediately on unequip, weapon swap,
death or other equipment removal. Refuse equipping when fewer than 80 points
are available after the equipment swap; keep the old equipment/prayers intact.
Recheck affordability before every shot if Worship or equipment bonuses change.
Never leave a weapon firing when the reserve cannot be funded. Handle login
restoration and prayer-book changes through the same authoritative rules.

Ammunition-slot bolts must contribute no extra offense, poison, resource cost
or recovery while a god crossbow is equipped. The same applies to inventory
ammunition in legacy inventory-equipment mode. Ordinary crossbows retain all
existing ammo rules. Do not implement this as a real/free Mithril bolt: doing
so risks spawning recoverable bolts or consuming unrelated equipped ammo.
Continue to use normal ranged critical, stagger, slow, PvP eligibility, XP,
projectile collision and hit-resolution systems.

Reward acquisition
------------------
GodArtifacts currently requires 80 Worship, 800 devotion, matching prayer book,
and an unclaimed reward. It spends 400 devotion when a reward is successfully
received. Add each new crossbow as that god's fourth artifact, preserving the
existing per-item claim keys. Players who claimed all three older artifacts
must become eligible for the crossbow. Preserve full-inventory failure safety
and existing reward dialogue. Recommend untradeable artifacts like god maces.
No new crafting or blessing recipe is implied by the request.
