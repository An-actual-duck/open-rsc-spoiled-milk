Underground neutral lighting — private-preview candidate, 2026-11-08

Owner policy
------------
Every authoritative signed level <0 receives steady neutral daytime lighting.
Level0 and all positive levels retain the current outside policy. The server
clock, location authority, protocol, map, render settings and animations are not
changed. Player brightness, material colors, terrain/object relief, RELIEF_AO,
glow emission and ordinary UI coloring remain available. Underground sunlight
cast shadows and sky backdrop are suppressed; lighting still models shapes.

Implementation
--------------
Scene publishes the signed level in a final Renderer3DFrame field, separate from
activePlane (which is a clamped compatibility/roof plane). Gameplay captures it
from PacketHandler.getLogicalWorldLevel(compatibilityY): native context preserves
signed deeper levels and legacy944-band3 correctly maps to−1. It deliberately
does not use the editor helper or infer negative levels from roof/chunk planes.
Capture occurs inside assembled gameplay, after the activation retain/loading
branches. Same-scope retained frames keep their level; cross-scope activation
uses the existing loading cover and clears old presentation. Previously queued
frames remain immutable. Login scene captures and protocol-session resets clear
the producer level to0; old constructors still default to0 for compatibility.

OpenGLFramePresenter binds the dequeued immutable frame in RendererFrameLighting,
a render-thread-local scope, and always restores the previous binding in finally.
There is no mutable global player level, no inherited thread scope and no allocated
scope wrapper. ThreadLocal removal may allocate a small map entry on the next
bind; this is not a zero-allocation claim. The common presentation and modeling-light getters
therefore cover resident/projected shader uniforms, fixed-function mesh/fallback
materials, cache brightness keys, world/entity sprite tint and fog consistently.
UI draw colors/settings are unchanged; null/login frames cannot inherit a prior
negative frame. Exceptions/context failures also restore the previous scope.

Underground presentation is the existing neutral DAY baseline with saved player
brightness. Modeling light is fixed at azimuth135/elevation45 degrees so slopes
and normals retain definition without celestial motion. Ambient/intensity,
relief, diagnostic color settings and glow paths remain unchanged. Both geometry
sunlight shadows and the old terrain-mask fallback are explicitly guarded by the
frame's signed level, including suspend/history invalidation on entry. Going
back outside starts fresh shadows through the existing lifecycle. Underground
sky uses the authoritative negative sign; existing >=0 sky heuristics are left
unchanged (their broader positive-floor behavior is outside this task).

Fog choice: DAY retains its existing pale-blue daylight-derived fog color, with
the preexisting black underground background and no visible sky. This preserves
the old daytime underground palette rather than inventing a new cave palette.
The manager/owner native preview should assess distant fog against that black
background. No claim is made that moving animated scenes become byte-identical:
intentional texture/effect animation and optional old underground flicker remain.
