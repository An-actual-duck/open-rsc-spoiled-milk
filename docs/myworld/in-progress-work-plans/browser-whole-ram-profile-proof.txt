Decision and limits
--------------------
Prepared wall/roof publication and canonical query products are justified targets.
Acceptance must demonstrate that actual World.loadSections consumes them and
skips the corresponding construction, while preserving local collision/picking,
minimap, origins and mutable model ownership. Source-only preload is insufficient.
Outside-silence samples include walking, Bridge work and transition tails, not a
pure stationary control. Endpoint and inclusive shares overlap and cannot be
summed. Profile1 without source-body export is preserved but not used for Java
attribution. No stale generated label, causal FPS forecast, RAM readiness or
whole-zone browser allocation claim is made by this report.
