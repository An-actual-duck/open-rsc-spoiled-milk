opaque T clear can remain a logical uniform zero until borrow; a fresh world

retain the original results. Turning the optimization off materializes pending
data; frame/epoch/surface reset and retained fades preserve ownership/tokens.

An explicit producer receipt after actual blackScreen RGB clearing can let
begin skip the second RGB fill. Every RGB coverage writer invalidates that
receipt; unknown external writers invalidate coverage, reset and resize cannot
reuse it, and fresh frame invalidates old proof. No draw is omitted.

paths and callers without browser ownership retain ordinary behavior.
