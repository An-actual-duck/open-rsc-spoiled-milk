Whole-overworld browser experiment — November 11, 2026
======================================================
PRIVATE / DEFAULT OFF. The complete GPU catalogue worked on the tested host.
Walking pauses improved, but stationary rendering regressed. This is experimental
evidence, not adoption, seamless travel, full classic CPU residency or 60 FPS.

Measured results
-----------------
Primary original-walking pair: walking-on-3 versus walking-off-2. Both used the
same frozen artifact/options and original minimap pointer input, with only
wholezone toggled. Server authority confirmed the route from (111,625,0), across
y=623/624, completing six boundary epochs (1 to 7) without load-report poll gaps.
Preparatory distant admin teleports are excluded. The final four crossings,
after approach and first out/return priming, measured:
                                  ON                       OFF
  New-frame gaps (ms)              585,436,552,432           707,3399,1495,709
  Mean warm gap                   501.25 ms                1,577.5 ms
  Whole route elapsed             12.478 s                 17.554 s
  Held images over route          4                        118
  Largest new-frame gap           1,384 ms                 3,399 ms

Mean warm gap fell 68.23%, but roughly 0.4–0.6 second warm pauses remain. Route
elapsed includes input/polling/settling, not pure CPU time or steady FPS. Per-leg
new interval tails distinguish fresh gaps from overlapping historical maxima;
held images do not count as fresh world frames. This is one primary pair, not a
randomized or multi-device estimate. walking-on-2 is preserved but not pooled.

Accepted stationary-on-2/off-1 samples used the same camera pose/source/settings,
were visible, focused and ready, and had no held frames during each 12 s sample:
                                  ON                       OFF
  Fresh frames / elapsed          517 / 12.071 s           614 / 12.085 s
  Fresh world FPS                 42.83                    50.81
  Observed draw calls             412                      379
  Observed triangles              463,882                  464,554

Fresh world FPS regressed 15.70%. The nominal 60 FPS Java/UI counter is not proof
of fresh world FPS. Backend/Worker frame completion is not physical scanout or a
GPU fence each frame. Draw/triangle counts do not establish GPU-time causation.

Remaining gates and next step
------------------------------
Actual door/tree changes, full GPU image/roof/indoor parity, forced context loss,
long soak and broader traversal/device acceptance remain open. The 43 dynamic/
signed Java unit cases and representative geometry oracles are separate evidence.
Selected upper native pages can differ from normal legacy fallback, so exact
full-scene ON/OFF pixels must not be assumed. Normal stop/disposal and OS release
were exercised. No public deployment or default change follows from this test.

Profile stationary ON/OFF before choosing a fix. Java per-cell clones, scans,
string/map/JSON lookup and material sorting, plus changed draw/shadow submission
counts, are source-backed hypotheses, not proven causes of the regression. Then
profile remaining local collision/pick/region construction, including held returns
and pre-bridge time. Preserve visuals/fog while measuring. The earlier
browser-modern-streaming-plan.txt remains saved and deferred pending review;
its reuse, budgets, background preparation, compact formats and later kernel/
instancing options are not rejected or automatically authorized by this report.
