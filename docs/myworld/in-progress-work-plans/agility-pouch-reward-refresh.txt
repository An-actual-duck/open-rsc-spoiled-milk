Agility pouch ordinary reward refresh — 2026-11-15

Approved implementation
The maintained reward definitions are server/conf/server/defs/extras/
AgilityPouchRewards.json. Ordinary categories are selected without replacement:
2–4 categories for tier 1/2, 3–4 for tier 3. Weights total 100:
ammunition 25, runes 15, empowered sigils 15, ingredients 10, ore 8,
logs 8, herbs 8, gems 5, food 3, full-dose potions 3.

Ammo chooses its family before its metal, so arrows, bolts, darts, throwing
knives and shuriken all have meaningful representation. Small cannonball
stacks are additional uncommon ammo outcomes in tiers 2/3. No poisoned ammo.
Life runes appear in all tiers. All five empowered sigil alignments appear;
tier 1 uses stone, tier 2 stone/silver, tier 3 stone/silver/moonsilver.
Resources and potion quantities are smaller than the retired tables. Current
Brawn, Deftness and Insight potion names/IDs are used rather than the misleading
legacy attack/restoration enum names. No separate prayer-restoration potion
exists in the current item catalogue. Full entries, quantities and material
weights are explicit in the JSON source.

Coating ingredients
Tier 3 ordinary Ingredients includes one raw Naga scale, Sanguinary gland,
Sticky saliva gland or Cockatrice eye, each weight 2 out of 92. These are not
an extra 1/2,000 bonus roll. Averaging over the 3/4 category count and weighted
selection without replacement, each is approximately 1 in 122 tier 3 chests.
None appears in tiers 1/2. Naga NPC scale drops now use base 1/128 with the
shared rare-component contribution/wealth rules, matching the other three.
Grinding and potion recipes are unchanged.

Major Slayer components (3343–3348) are excluded, including Lightning Horn
and Abyssal Vertibrae. No generic rare drop table is invoked by the pouches.
Existing utility unique rolls and duplicate replacements are unchanged.

Reliability and validation
The immutable catalog validates schema, nesting, IDs, positive weights,
quantities and tier identity before a chest is consumed. Existing normal
inventory delivery/overflow behavior remains in use. A deterministic offline
fixture checks every leaf at its quantity endpoints against real item
catalogues, category selection, live plugin chest consumption and refusal of
invalid configuration before consumption. Evidence is under the declared
output/tests/agility-pouch-reward-refresh category. Naga scales and utility
fixtures additionally verify rarity, grinding, movement, persistence and client
bindings. No public server or live player data is used by these tests.

Activation
This change is prepared for a later release; no public deployment or restart
is included in this task. The latest user-authored map is a separate integration.
