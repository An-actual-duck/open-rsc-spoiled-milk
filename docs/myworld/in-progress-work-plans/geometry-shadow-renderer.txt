Behavior and ownership
- Real resident VBO/material-index geometry casts directional depth shadows;
  terrain, walls, available roofs and scenery receive them in the existing
  Remaster shader. Alpha-cutout coverage uses the visible material atlas.
  Camera visibility/frustum/fog culling does not exclude light-frustum casters.
- High uses a 2048 near map with 32-tile half extent and 3x3 comparison taps,
  plus a 2048 static far map with 96-tile half extent. Low uses a 1024 near map
  with 48-tile half extent and one hardware comparison tap. Near updates every
  rendered frame and includes animated scenery. Off performs no shadow pass.
- The far projection/texture commit together. Static geometry/material alpha,
  four tiles of center movement, 0.08 degrees of sun motion, or 150 rendered
  frames invalidate it. These are invalidation conditions, not a promise of
  rare rebuilds: actual moving-sun/scene costs are reported by the benchmark.
- Texel snapping anchors the sun projection across presentation-origin rebases.
  Geometry is Y-down; sun conversion is explicit. ALL submitted native halo
  and object vertices already share the primary presentation basis. Their cell
  origins identify residency, not another vertex translation. A shared shadow
  anchor fixes double offsets; separate shadow uniforms preserve pre-existing
  terrain material-noise coordinates. No map/scene authority gate was changed.
- A captured Seers road depression has a 90-world-unit height change and a
  1.31-degree grazing sun angle. Sun-facing bias-normal flipping leaked up to
  17/255 false sunlight across genuinely occluded faces. Terrain now uses its
  outward physical-up normal and retains zero direct sun on back-facing faces.
  A maintained matching 40-triangle GPU replay changed 112 affected samples
  from a failing maximum 17/255 to exactly 0; exposed adjacent road stays 255.
  All terrain casters remain. Prop/wall bias, wrapped material lighting,
  ambient, independent glow and day/night ownership remain unchanged.
  Falsified gradient/precision experiments are preserved only in ignored proof
  artifacts, not maintained source.
- The owner's additional indoor policy uses only declared positive roof bits.
  A cached one-texel-per-tile mask suppresses direct sun uniformly on covered
  TERRAIN, GAME_OBJECT and WALL_OBJECT receivers on the mask's matching plane.
  Thus separate object sun shadows do not appear on an indoor floor, including
  when global Hide Roofs omits visible roof meshes. WORLD WALL facades and ROOF
  surfaces are exempt. No wall-flood inference, custom IDs or guessed heights
  are used. Roof-bit source-window origins retain their metadata authority.
- The mask is 2D. An unusual ground-plane prop protruding above a roof still
  counts as covered; there is no authored roof-height authority to split it.
  Higher-plane receivers do not inherit the lower-plane mask. Exposed roof
  model kinds continue normal shadow sampling. This deliberately bounded
  policy preserves exterior facades and rooftop geometry.
- Submitted camera-hidden roof meshes can cast physically; global Hide Roofs
  can omit those meshes before rendering. The declared-bit interior policy
  works without importing missing geometry or changing loader/readiness.
- GL 3 FBO/VAO support is optional. Unsupported capability, depth-program failure
  or runtime pass failure explicitly falls back to the prior ground mask.
  That fallback retains its existing approximate policy. Bounded textures may
  remain allocated while Off; Low releases the far map, and relog/session/context
  cleanup releases depth and roof-mask resources. Classic is unchanged.
- Sprite-shaped actors do not cast new geometry silhouettes. Animated scenery
  is present only in the near shadow map. This milestone is not complete
  character-shadow support or a general renderer/asset rewrite.

Player settings
Off/Low/High are saved as shadow_quality. Runtime diagnostic override:
spoiledmilk.shadowQuality / SPOILED_MILK_SHADOW_QUALITY. Existing Classic settings
without the new key migrate to Off. Profile defaults are Classic Off / Remaster
High, while explicit runtime overrides remain authoritative.

The optional GPU runner compiles pixel fixtures, takes the shared GPU flock and
writes logs/PNGs under output/tests/geometry-shadow-renderer/gpu-<mode>-<run>.
It creates unfocused hidden windows, never installs input/grabs and never logs
into a server. It runs both generic geometry/state and matching real-terrain
shape regressions. The unsupported variant requests Mesa GL 2.1; capability-only
proof is distinct from a complete native-client fallback route.

Separate scenery lifecycle fix
Native testing exposed an inherited same-position teleport defect: local object
and wall delivery sets were cleared but static_scene_scan_key stayed valid, so
unchanged location/world versions skipped the required resend. Commit 7cba3d710
adds the owned GameStateUpdater invalidation API at Player.resetLocalObjectState.
It does not change baseline authority, summaries or unrelated player attributes.
Old production reset/cache methods failed the dynamic regression; fixed methods
pass steady/version/region/signed-level cases. Fresh normal-login native proof
retains all 935 objects / 29 walls through a same-coordinate hard region reload.

Native evidence / acceptance
Final candidate12 matched legacy/Low/High, moving-camera, sun-motion, maximum-
distance, roof/day/night/water/relog routes are being collected by ai-3. Do not
substitute superseded measurements: candidate1 had incorrect object offsets and
zero actual projectile actors despite requested emitters. Candidate9 still had

records raw framebuffer, content viewport and internal/UI source separately.
Resident GL world geometry rasterizes at the physical content viewport; internal
960x540 describes legacy/UI/projection coordinates, not a half-resolution world.

GPU render time, CPU submission/scene time, completed frame intervals and loading
transitions must be reported separately with median/p95/p99 and misses. Hidden
throughput is not compositor delivery. No strict all-frame 60 FPS claim, final
owner-visible cadence acceptance or all-location guarantee has been made.
