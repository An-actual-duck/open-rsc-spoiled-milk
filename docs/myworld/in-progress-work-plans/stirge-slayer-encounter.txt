Stirge encounter — initial implementation
=======================================
Owner approvals: completed five flight directions and three-pose profile attack,
including bat-gray wings. Canonical approved source is under assets/authoring/
monsters/stirge/plunge-attack. Initial can/gland icons are presented for review.

Stats and identity
NPC 882, Stirge, combat 55, 60 HP, legacy Att/Str/Def 60/50/50.
Modern melee offense 55, melee/ranged/magic defense 50/80/50. Melee only.
Base maximum melee damage is 8 before any external modifiers. Passive until
attacked, 30-second respawn. World placement is explicitly reserved for owner.

Bleed and drinking
Every positive, unsuppressed primary melee hit applies a wound without the
player's 70%-of-max-roll rule. Per-source/per-target wound ownership is retained.
Subsequent hits raise severity 1 -> 2 -> 3 (cap) and refresh three pulses,
without adding another simultaneous wound or postponing the next scheduled
pulse. Shared bleed cadence: every three game ticks; pulse ranges 1–3, 3–5,
5–7 respectively. Wounds expire after three pulses without another hit.
The originating Stirge heals 100% of actual delivered bleed damage, capped at
its maximum health. Direct melee damage does not heal it. Dead/despawned/
respawned sources, dead/logged-out targets and spatial-domain changes invalidate
wounds. Player bleed, Cosmic luck, PvP legality and provenance remain intact.

Bug Off
3462 full three-use can; 3463 two sprays; 3464 one spray; 3465 empty can.
Spray uses exact inventory replacement, returns an empty can on the third use,
and applies ten minutes of protection. This initial duration follows existing
Slayer counters and can be tuned. Examine: "Keeps pesky stirges from drinking
your blood." Protection prevents new Stirge wounds and immediately removes
existing Stirge-owned wounds, stopping their healing. It does not remove other
players' bleed or alter poison/burn. An active item status shows its timer.

Slayer
Bran, Brimhaven Veteran tier: 25 Stirges for 12 Veteran points, inserted before
Nagas, both mandatory and repeatable. BLOOD_DRINKING hazard explains preparation
and defenses. First applicable assignment grants one full can through the
existing first-only supply system; later assignments/reminders do not duplicate
it. All Slayer suppliers sell full cans for one Veteran point, initial stock
20/restock two. Existing backpack costs remain unchanged.

Roster v1–v5 remain historical inputs. New v6 activates only when the world's
spawn catalog contains NPC 882, so no one is assigned an unplaced monster.
Migration preserves earned rank, currency, partial task credit and inventory
upgrades; completed higher ranks are grandfathered per existing policy. Keep
at least one Stirge placement after v6 goes live: removing the entire family
would be a roster rollback, which the existing progression policy rejects.

Drops and crafting
3466 Sanguinary gland, independent base 1/128 component roll, using existing
rare-component luck and contribution rules. Ordinary loot uses the standard
128-weight budget: coins, air/water runes, tarromin/harralander, shared rare
access and empty outcomes. The anti-coagulant's recipe is still undecided;
this change supplies the ingredient, not an invented herb/XP recipe.

Art/runtime integration
Runtime atlas is six 210-wide direction columns, three 160-high rows, containing
all eighteen approved native poses. Flight PNGs are copied at +30,+15 within
runtime cells; attack PNGs at +0,+0. No rescaling/repainting of approved pixels.
Presentation scale is 1.5 world units per native pixel, consistent with the
bat reference. New animation appends after every published equipment alias,
so god crossbows, coated axes and other published appearances retain IDs.
Can/gland sources and Aseprite masters are under assets/authoring/items; only
native 48x32 binary-alpha exports are in runtime. Generation inputs preserved.
