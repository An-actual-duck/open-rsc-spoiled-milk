Task: audit agility obstacles and shortcuts involving underground or upper
floors, including surface-origin routes and success, failure and intermediate
landings. Branch: fix/agility-nonzero-layer-landings. Source base:

RESULT

Confirmed layered-coordinate defects are fixed in Barbarian ledge falls,
Legends cave stair direction tests, Shilo cave rocks/rubble direction tests,
Shilo surface exits, Digsite rope ascents, and lava-crossing facing targets.
Other packed destinations in those agility routes now explicitly state their
signed floor. Already-correct Gnome and Underground Pass controls remain intact.
Requirements, odds, XP, damage, messages and original movement type are retained.

The two-coordinate teleport overload preserves a native player's current level
for ordinary logical Y, whereas a legacy packed underground/upper-floor Y can
still decode correctly. Consequently this audit does not treat every old packed
teleport as a reproduced defect. Old packed directional predicates do fail on
native logical Y. Existing legacyPackedY(entity) keeps those comparisons valid
for both native and classic coordinates; explicit x/y/level teleport overloads
also retain their classic projection. No loader or global teleport API changed.
