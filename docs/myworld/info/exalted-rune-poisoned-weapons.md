# Exalted Rune Poisoned Weapons

Exalted Rune follows the established poison eligibility boundary: only the
Dagger and Spear have poisoned variants. Weapon poison uses the exact-name
conversion (`Poisoned ` plus the original item name), consumes one weapon
poison and one weapon, and produces the corresponding item below.

| Original | Poisoned item | ID |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Exalted Rune dagger | Poisoned Exalted Rune dagger | 3309 |
| Exalted Rune Spear | Poisoned Exalted Rune Spear | 3310 |

Both variants preserve the original weapon's stats, level requirement, value,
inventory silhouette, equipped appearance, equipment slot, members/note flags,
and handedness. They are tier-12 poison weapons: a successful poison proc
applies 48 poison power into a 120-power weapon ceiling under the shared
weapon-poison proc rules.

All other Exalted Rune equipment intentionally has no poisoned variant. This
includes the remaining combat weapons as well as the Hatchet, Pickaxe, and
Shears gathering tools.
