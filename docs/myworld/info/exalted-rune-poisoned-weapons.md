# Exalted Rune Poisoned Weapons

Exalted Rune has poisoned variants for every combat weapon in the smithing
family. Weapon poison uses the established exact-name conversion (`Poisoned `
plus the original item name), consumes one weapon poison and one weapon, and
produces the corresponding item below.

| Original | Poisoned item | ID |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Exalted Rune dagger | Poisoned Exalted Rune dagger | 3309 |
| Exalted Rune Short Sword | Poisoned Exalted Rune Short Sword | 3310 |
| Exalted Rune Long Sword | Poisoned Exalted Rune Long Sword | 3311 |
| Exalted Rune Scimitar | Poisoned Exalted Rune Scimitar | 3312 |
| Exalted Rune 2-handed Sword | Poisoned Exalted Rune 2-handed Sword | 3313 |
| Exalted Rune battle Axe | Poisoned Exalted Rune battle Axe | 3314 |
| Exalted Rune Scythe | Poisoned Exalted Rune Scythe | 3315 |
| Exalted Rune Mace | Poisoned Exalted Rune Mace | 3316 |
| Exalted Rune Spear | Poisoned Exalted Rune Spear | 3317 |

Each variant preserves the original weapon's stats, level requirement, value,
inventory silhouette, equipped appearance, equipment slot, members/note flags,
and handedness. The poisoned Scythe remains a sweeping AoE Scythe. These are
tier-12 poison weapons: a successful poison proc applies 48 poison power into a
120-power weapon ceiling under the shared weapon-poison proc rules.

The Exalted Rune Hatchet, Pickaxe, and Shears are gathering tools rather than
combat weapons. They intentionally have no poisoned variants and remain on
their existing Woodcutting, Mining, and Harvesting paths.
