Rebirth goblin A style and recreation notes
Owner-selected reference, 2026-11-17

OWNER'S DIRECTION
Clearly pixel art, without excessive polish or an extremely blocky appearance.
Retain some of RSC's crude handmade quality while allowing additional detail.
The owner chose A from three front-facing concepts because it had less detail
and simpler shading. This is a newly designed goblin, not an upscale of RSC art.
The second diagonal-away still was accepted as a successful on-model direction.
The Rebirth concept remains speculative; these choices do not authorize replacing
classic artwork or implementing a new renderer/world.

ASSISTANT'S ORIGINAL PROMPT ELABORATION
The exact submitted A prompt is preserved in v1/selected-a-prompt.txt. It specified:
- Earthy, primitive early computer RPG character art; rough handmade charm.
- Broad irregular pixel clusters, slightly lumpy anatomy, understated face.
- No continuous dark contour; dark green shadow planes define the silhouette.
- Approximately 16-20 opaque colors, two or three rough shades per material.
- Readable hands, face and knees without slick highlights or decoration.
- Moss-green skin, big pointed ears, angular long nose, slightly hunched shoulders,
  wiry arms, broad hands/bare feet, ragged brown waistcloth and rope belt.
- Bare chest, no weapon/props; front-facing, arms slightly away, planted feet.
- Crisp stepped edges; no antialiasing, gradients, blur or subpixel detail.
These were added implementation choices in the prompt, not independently supplied
requirements from the owner. The selected result is the primary style evidence.
Do not apply goblin-specific anatomy, green skin or clothing to unrelated NPCs.

ACTUAL PREPARATION
The prompt requested an 8x enlargement of a native 128px design. The returned
source was 1254x1254, not the requested 1024x1024; it was not used as native art.
Aseprite Lua found the opaque bounds (alpha >=128), resampled nearest-neighbor to
104px visible height, centered it in a 128x128 canvas, set top to14 and baseline
to118 exclusive, and quantized with no dithering. A ended with19 opaque colors.
Use the recorded masters/recipes and actual palette, not the requested source
dimensions as a guarantee about image-generator output.

RESUME THE STYLE
1. Inspect the selected front PNG/master at native and enlarged size.
2. Supply that original as the identity/style reference for further views.
3. Describe direction in screen terms, with head/torso/pelvis/feet all aligned.
4. Establish separate appendage roots and near/far depth in a construction guide.
5. Use multidirectional-sprites for independent anatomy and identity review.
6. Only propagate a reviewed static pose into animation; compare ordered frames
   and playback, then clean pixels. A file-format pass is not an anatomy pass.
7. Keep approved original artwork unchanged; save new candidates separately.

For another creature, reuse the economical cluster/shading treatment, native
pixel-density target and rough finish; create species-specific proportions,
materials, palette and landmarks. Avoid drifting toward glossy illustration,
dense texture/noisy dithering, continuous black ink outlines or chibi proportions.

ANIMATION LESSONS
The front test used8 distinct frames at110ms/frame (880ms loop), without idle.
It remains exploratory: owner noted a separating forehead and back-foot artifacts.
This timing is an experiment, not an engine/gameplay requirement.
The rejected first away cycle showed why palette and technical validation are
insufficient: it mixed facing directions and ear/hip attachments. Rejected poses
must not become identity references simply because they have more completed frames.
The accepted away-static-v2 has no completed walk animation.

REPRODUCTION LIMIT
Exact prompts here are the text submitted through built-in image_gen. They do
not expose hidden model instructions, and no seed or fixed model-build identifier
was recorded. This package preserves the actual images and editable native
masters so continuation does not rely on stochastic text-only regeneration.
