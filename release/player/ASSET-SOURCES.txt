SPOILED MILK VISUAL ASSET SOURCES
=================================

Added runtime animation assets use Pimen source assets. Pimen has confirmed
distribution is permitted with source code available.

Original additions by the project author include the fishing rod equipment
sprites.

ThatKidBobo supplied the remastered player, NPC, inventory/ground item, and
equipment sprite artwork included by the optional remastered-sprite catalog.
The optional remastered-sprite option is temporarily hidden and disabled at
the owner's request. The sources, historical credits and catalog are preserved.
Per-set mappings and provenance are tracked under
dev/myworld/assets/authoring/remastered-sprites/.

Next-update gem-dragon, sigil, coating and well artwork includes project-made
additions and adaptations of existing RuneScape Classic artwork. Provenance and
approved asset receipts are recorded under dev/myworld/assets/runtime/sprites/npcs/gem-dragons/,
dev/myworld/assets/authoring/items/moonsilver-sigils/, dev/myworld/assets/authoring/items/blight-sigil/,
dev/myworld/assets/authoring/equipment/battleaxe-coatings/, dev/myworld/assets/authoring/items/coating-reagents/ and
dev/myworld/assets/authoring/world/cleric-wells/. The tower's project-authored scenery exports are
recorded in the corresponding native model reviews under docs/myworld/in-progress-work-plans/.
Adaptations retain the underlying game's provenance and redistribution
constraints; project authorship and the source-code licence do not grant new
rights to Jagex's base artwork.

The twenty final Cleric spell icons are by Atelier Pixerellia, as identified
by the project owner. Exact supplied PNGs and their attribution are recorded in
dev/myworld/assets/runtime/ui/cleric/PROVENANCE.json. This artist credit does
not assert a new licence grant; existing asset terms remain applicable.

Prayer UI icon assets are sourced from a royalty-free repository.

Summon UI icon provenance is tracked in dev/myworld/assets/CREDITS.txt.

Source:
- https://pimen.itch.io/

CraftPix-derived icon and Phoenix/Kraken animation assets are not included in
this downloadable client or source asset tree.
