SPOILED MILK — NEXT UPDATE (DRAFT)

PRIVATE ACCEPTANCE DRAFT — not published or final gameplay acceptance.
Release version and alpha/beta label will be chosen after final testing. Update your client before logging in when this release goes live.

Slayer Tower
A fully decorated tower brings the new Slayer encounters together. Associates control initial entry and access to higher floors; their rooms provide a safe place to talk. Players can freely return down the stairs and leave.

Gem dragons beneath the Crafting Guild
The caves require level 70 Crafting. Five gem dragon varieties progress from sapphire to dragonstone. Each drops dragon bones and rolls guaranteed uncut gems, alongside a normal loot table focused on crafting supplies, bolts, runes, coins and rare Dragon Metal Scrap. Gem cutter gloves prevent gem-crushing failures; the Gem cutter’s monocle gives a 5% chance of an extra cut gem.

Slow, coatings and antidotes
Battleaxes can receive a permanent, single-dose Viscous coating, with an option to wipe it clean. Shared Slow and weaker poison contributions work with the existing combat systems. New spider-egg ingredients and antidotes provide more gathering and cleansing options.

Cleric overhaul
Three growing blessing and curse tiers for Saradomin, Guthix and Zamorak combine their effects as stronger tiers are unlocked. Zamorak's curses expose enemies to critical hits, Stagger and executions; bosses and protected encounters cannot be executed. Unify and Greater Unify help parties regroup. Holy Power controls spell strength within each tier. The in-game guide explains effects, power thresholds and costs.

Grace, Blight and Moonsilver
Existing neutral sigils become Grace without replacing players’ stocks. Charge Grace at Divine wellsprings and Blight at Ichor wells; god sigils still use their god altars. Silver and Moonsilver sigils start from moulded blanks, producing three blanks per bar before carving and infusion. Spell costs now use the appropriate combination of sigil grades.

Combat presentation and equipment
The revised floating hit numbers and their settings are included. Cosmic robes improve combat luck by giving eligible combat rolls another chance; their old critical-hit bonus is replaced. Shared Stagger prevents attacks and provides a common immunity window.

Reliability
Auction transactions now handle database failures and reconnects more safely. World navigation and NPC visibility have additional fixes for changing levels and reconnecting underground. The final map includes reviewed shoreline, ladder-return and isolated floor-colour repairs.

Known minor visual limitations
Four existing grey floor joins remain flagged because the current ground-colour palette cannot reproduce their intended grey closely. A broader system for automatic floor-edge matching and separate half-tile materials is deferred.

Not part of this release
Mystic Blue Dragon, Fierce Red Dragon, Corporeal Beast, Ante and Cleric Tier 4 remain future work. The development editor remains a separate private pre-alpha tool.

Final artwork
The owner-selected Cleric spell icons distinguish each of the twenty spells. Final combined visual acceptance remains part of private testing.
