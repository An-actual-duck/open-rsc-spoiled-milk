SPOILED MILK - @VERSION@
=======================

Launch with play-spoiled-milk.sh on Linux or macOS.
Launch with Play Spoiled Milk.cmd on Windows.

Choose a launcher ending in World Chunks for traditional loading or
World Streaming for smoother travel on a compatible server. On Linux,
the named .sh launchers use world-chunks and world-streaming in their filenames.
On macOS, use the named .command launchers. The original launchers open
World Chunks. All launchers automatically check for updates before starting.
